Retriangulation Redrawn

Every pair of standing OS trig pillars in Great Britain with a clear line of sight over the terrain.

    Terrain only. Lines clear the bare ground with standard refraction. Trees and buildings are not modelled, and in lowland England most of these lines are probably blocked today.

    How this was made

    Pillars are the 6,079 standing pillars in the OS Complete Trig Archive. Sight height is the archive height of the flush bracket, raised to the pillar top (1.22 m above ground) plus 0.25 m for an instrument.

    Each pair up to 150 km apart was tested against OS Terrain 50, a 50 m bare-earth model. The test allows for Earth curvature and a refraction coefficient of 0.13, ignoring 100 m at each end. 229,330 pairs are clear.

    Checked against the 641 pillar-to-pillar rays the surveyors actually observed between 1936 and 1962, the model finds 95.5% of them.
